align-node: motor node (milestone M4), PROVISIONAL pins for an Adafruit Feather ESP32-C6

Motors:      one stepper channel, 0: ULN2003 IN1 to IN4 on GPIO 3, 2, 21, 22 (Feather A4, A5, SCK, MO).
             All four start low (coils off) and stay low except during a move. Channel 1 is unpopulated.
             A move needs a received command AND the permit input high AND the rail input high:
             - from the bus: a MOVE_REL, a coordinator heartbeat under 300 ms old carrying protocol version 2,
               an INHIBIT 0 from the coordinator, and the channel's soft limits and direction sign set by CONFIG.
               Boots inhibited.
             - from the console: `bench on`, then `jog` or `torque`, only while no coordinator is heard and no
               ESTOP is latched (an INHIBIT 0 clears it).
             At most 4000 steps and 25 s per move; one channel at a time; coils released after every move.
Motor rail:  GPIO 0 carries a 1 kHz arm pulse only while a move runs; low otherwise. GPIO 7 reads the rail.
Permit:      GPIO 6 reads the motor permit, pulled down: unconnected means no permit and no move.
Bus:         TWAI on GPIO 23 alone (Feather MI; TX and RX looped back, open drain, pull-up), 250 kbit/s,
             self-test mode, no transceiver. It sends a heartbeat every 100 ms and a status every second, and acts
             on frames addressed to node 1.
Other I/O:   none. Every other GPIO stays in its reset state, among them the Feather's RGB LED and I2C power
             (GPIO 20), its I2C pins (18, 19), and its LED (15).
Flash:       writes a 9-byte position record per channel to the `nvs` partition before every move (marked
             suspect) and after it. If that partition holds data it cannot read (for example ESP-IDF NVS from an
             earlier image), it ERASES the partition and writes every channel at position zero, marked suspect.
Watchdog:    the TIMG1 watchdog resets the chip if not fed for 1 s; a reset leaves every output low.

Bench wiring: ULN2003 IN1 to IN4 to Feather A4, A5, SCK, MO; its + to USB (5 V) and - to GND.
             Jumper Feather 6 and 7 to 3V (never to USB or BAT: the inputs are not 5 V tolerant).
With no switched rail on the bench, the firmware checks are the only layer between a command and the motor.
