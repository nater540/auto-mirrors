align-node: motor node (milestone M4), PROVISIONAL pins for a bare ESP32-C6 development board

Motors:      two stepper channels. Channel 0 drives GPIO 18, 19, 20, 21; channel 1 drives GPIO 10, 11, 2, 3
             (ULN2003 IN1 to IN4). All eight start low (coils off) and stay low except during a move.
             A move needs a received command AND the permit input high AND the rail input high:
             - from the bus: a MOVE_REL, a coordinator heartbeat under 300 ms old, an INHIBIT 0 from the
               coordinator, and the channel's soft limits and direction sign set by CONFIG. Boots inhibited.
             - from the console: `bench on`, then `jog` or `torque`. No heartbeat or CONFIG is needed on the bench.
             At most 4000 steps and 25 s per move; one channel at a time; coils released after every move.
Motor rail:  GPIO 0 carries a 1 kHz arm pulse only while a move runs; low otherwise. GPIO 7 reads the rail.
Permit:      GPIO 6 reads the motor permit, pulled down: unconnected means no permit and no move.
Bus:         TWAI on GPIO 22 alone (TX and RX looped back, open drain, pull-up), 250 kbit/s, self-test mode,
             no transceiver. It sends a heartbeat every 100 ms and a status every second, and acts on frames
             addressed to node 1. GPIO 23 stays in its reset state.
Other I/O:   none. Every other GPIO stays in its reset state.
Flash:       writes an 8-byte position record per channel to the `nvs` partition after every move. At first boot,
             if that partition holds data it cannot read (for example ESP-IDF NVS from an earlier image),
             it ERASES the partition and starts every channel at position zero, marked suspect.

Bench wiring: ULN2003 IN1 to IN4 to GPIO 18 to 21, its + to 5V and - to GND. Jumper GPIO 6 and GPIO 7 to 3V3.
With no switched rail on the bench, the firmware checks are the only layer between a command and the motor.
