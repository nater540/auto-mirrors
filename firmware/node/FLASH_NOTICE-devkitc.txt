align-node (devkitc): motor node (milestone M4), PROVISIONAL pins for an ESP32-C6-DevKitC-1, node 2 (mirror 2)

Motors:      two stepper channels. Channel 0: ULN2003 IN1 to IN4 on GPIO 21, 20, 19, 18 (header J3 pins 7 to 10).
             Channel 1: ULN2003 IN1 to IN4 on GPIO 10, 11, 2, 3 (header J1 pins 10 to 13).
             All eight start low (coils off) and stay low except during a move of their channel.
             A move needs a received command AND the permit input high AND the rail input high:
             - from the bus: a MOVE_REL to node 2, a coordinator heartbeat under 300 ms old carrying protocol
               version 2, an INHIBIT 0 from the coordinator, and the channel's soft limits and direction sign set
               by CONFIG. Boots inhibited.
             - from the console: `bench on`, then `jog` or `torque`, only while no coordinator is heard and no
               ESTOP is latched (an INHIBIT 0 clears it).
             At most 4000 steps and 25 s per move; one channel at a time; coils released after every move.
Motor rail:  GPIO 0 (J1 pin 7) carries a 1 kHz arm pulse only while a move runs; low otherwise. GPIO 7 (J1 pin 6)
             reads the rail.
Permit:      GPIO 6 (J1 pin 5) reads the motor permit, pulled down: unconnected means no permit and no move.
Bus:         TWAI through an SN65HVD230 transceiver: TX on GPIO 23 (J3 pin 5) to CTX, RX on GPIO 22 (J3 pin 6)
             from CRX. 250 kbit/s, normal mode: a frame needs another node to acknowledge it. It sends a heartbeat
             every 100 ms and a status of each channel every second, and acts on frames addressed to node 2.
Other I/O:   none. Every other GPIO stays in its reset state, among them the RGB LED (GPIO 8), the Boot button
             (GPIO 9), and the spare GPIO 1.
Flash:       writes a 9-byte position record per channel to the `nvs` partition before every move (marked
             suspect), after it, when a CONFIG changes the channel's direction sign (the position converted), and
             on the console's `zero` (bench mode, no coordinator heard; nothing moves). A failed write stops a move
             that has not stepped yet and refuses moves until it lands; it is written again every second while no
             move runs. If that partition holds data it cannot read (for example ESP-IDF NVS from an earlier image),
             it ERASES the partition and writes every channel at position zero, marked suspect.
Watchdog:    the TIMG1 watchdog resets the chip if not fed for 1 s; a reset leaves every output low.

Bench wiring: each ULN2003's IN1 to IN4 to its channel's four pins above; its + to a 5 V supply that holds 5 V
             with a motor running (200 to 370 mA), its - to that supply's ground and to G.
             Jumper GPIO 6 and 7 to 3V3 (J1 pin 1; never to 5V: the inputs are not 5 V tolerant).
             SN65HVD230: 3V3 to 3V3, GND to G, CTX to GPIO 23, CRX to GPIO 22; CANH and CANL to the coordinator's.
             USB to the board's USB port (the console), not its UART port.
UNVERIFIED on this board: that no coil turns on through reset and boot (watch both drivers' LEDs), and that no
             32 kHz crystal is fitted on GPIO 0 and 1.
With no switched rail on the bench, the firmware checks are the only layer between a command and the motor.
