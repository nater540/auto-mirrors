align-coordinator: interlock and moves over the bus (milestone M5), PROVISIONAL pins for an Adafruit Feather ESP32-C6

Motors:      moved only by the console's `jog`, through the state machine of reference 7.3: in Idle with the node
             present, after 500 ms without a fire, the relay is energized; once its feedback reads the laser loop
             open, INHIBIT 0 goes out and then one MOVE_REL with `finish_plus` clear. When the node answers (or not,
             after 3 tries), INHIBIT 1 goes out and the relay is released. Every other INHIBIT says inhibited.
             A node that hears this heartbeat refuses console moves (bench moves need a silent bus).
             On every node that appears or restarts it sets each axis's soft limits (-4400 to 4400 steps) and
             direction sign (forward) by CONFIG.
Interlock:   GPIO 22 (Feather MO) drives the relay, high to energize; low at boot, at reset, and in every state but
             arming and moving. GPIO 21 (SCK) reads its feedback, pulled down, high when the laser loop is open.
             A fault (fire or job while armed, feedback disagreeing, a lost node) releases the relay at once and
             sends ESTOP.
Ruida I/O:   GPIO 3 (A4) reads Status, pulled up, low = job running; GPIO 2 (A5) reads fire sense, pulled up,
             low = firing. Both polarities UNVERIFIED (hand-off open item 9). FootSW is not driven.
Bus:         TWAI through an SN65HVD230 transceiver: TX on GPIO 23 (MI) to CTX, RX on GPIO 1 (A0) from CRX.
             250 kbit/s, normal mode. A heartbeat and the inhibit level every 100 ms, the inhibit at once on change.
Other I/O:   none. Every other GPIO stays in its reset state, among them the Feather's RGB LED and I2C power
             (GPIO 20), its I2C pins (18, 19), and its LED (15).
Flash:       nothing is written after boot.
Watchdog:    the TIMG1 watchdog resets the chip if not fed for 1 s; a reset releases the relay.

Bench wiring: SN65HVD230: 3V3 to 3V, GND to GND, CTX to MI, CRX to A0; CANH and CANL to the node's.
             Jumper MO to SCK (the relay's feedback follows its drive), and wire MO to the node's pin 6 in place of
             that pin's 3V jumper (MO is the motor permit). A4 and A5 open: no job, no fire.
With no relay on the bench, MO drives the node's permit directly: the firmware checks are the only layer.
