align-coordinator: interlock, moves, measurement, alignment, bring-up (M5, M6), PROVISIONAL pins for a Feather ESP32-C6

Motors:      moved only by the console's `jog`, `calibrate`, `align`, `poke`, `backlash`, and `hysteresis` (the last
             five run one move at a time, the axes started from the nodes' STATUS). `backlash` and `hysteresis` move
             one axis only, every move checked against the soft limits first, staying within 128 steps below and 90
             above where the axis started, and ending there. A poke, and a `backlash` or `hysteresis` that fails
             part way, moves the axis back to where it started, unless `stop` ended the procedure.
             Every move goes through the state machine of reference 7.3: in Idle with the node present, after
             500 ms without a fire, the relay is energized; once its feedback reads the laser loop open, in a later
             millisecond than the energize, INHIBIT 0 goes out and then one MOVE_REL with `finish_plus` clear. When
             the node answers (or not, after 3 tries), INHIBIT 1 goes out and the relay is released; an answer of a
             soft limit or a lost rail then faults. Every other INHIBIT says inhibited.
             While a procedure runs, the console's `jog` and `measure` are refused; a procedure starts only in
             Idle with no move or measurement asked for or running. `stop` during a procedure stops its move in
             flight and refuses its every later move, and faults the coordinator until `clear`.
             A node that hears this heartbeat refuses console moves (bench moves need a silent bus).
             On every node that appears or restarts it sets each axis's soft limits (-4800 to 4800 steps: the
             configured -4000 to 4000 widened by 10% of the travel) and direction sign (forward) by CONFIG.
Interlock:   GPIO 22 (Feather MO) drives the relay, high to energize; low at boot, at reset, and in every state but
             arming and moving. GPIO 21 (SCK) reads its feedback, pulled down, high when the laser loop is open.
             A fault (fire or job while armed, feedback disagreeing, a node's pin fault, lost rail or move timeout,
             a soft limit, CAN bus-off) releases the relay at once and sends ESTOP, again with every heartbeat until
             the console's `clear`. Losing an expected node, or the node of a move, releases the relay and sends
             INHIBIT 1, without ESTOP; a job it interrupts is still watched, and no move or job starts until that
             job's Status line falls after rising (or its timeout faults).
Ruida I/O:   GPIO 3 (A4) drives FootSW's optocoupler, high = active: high for up to 200 ms only for a measurement
             (`measure`, or one the procedure takes 200 ms after its last move) in Idle, with the relay released
             and the sensor node present. On the machine this starts the Ruida's job, which fires the laser. It goes
             low again as soon as the job stops waiting to start, and is low at boot, at reset, and otherwise; no
             move is armed while it is high. GPIO 2 (A5) reads Status, pulled up, low = job running; GPIO 7 reads
             fire sense, pulled up, low = firing, and latches each change of it by interrupt. Both polarities UNVERIFIED
             (hand-off open item 9). Each burst of fire is a SHOT to every node.
Bus:         TWAI through an SN65HVD230 transceiver: TX on GPIO 23 (MI) to CTX, RX on GPIO 1 (A0) from CRX.
             250 kbit/s, normal mode. A heartbeat and the inhibit level every 100 ms, the inhibit at once on change.
Other I/O:   none. Every other GPIO stays in its reset state, among them the Feather's RGB LED and I2C power
             (GPIO 20), its I2C pins (18, 19), and its LED (15).
Flash:       the nvs partition holds the calibration and the last good alignment (reference 3.6). At boot they are
             read; contents that do not read are erased, before the watchdog starts. A new calibration, and the
             screws' positions after an alignment that converged, are written after the procedure ends, with no
             move running: each flash operation masks interrupts and stalls the chip. Nothing else is written.
Watchdog:    the TIMG1 watchdog resets the chip if not fed for 1 s; a reset releases the relay and FootSW.

Bench wiring: SN65HVD230: 3V3 to 3V, GND to GND, CTX to MI, CRX to A0; CANH and CANL to the node's.
             Jumper MO to SCK (the relay's feedback follows its drive), and wire MO to the node's pin 6 in place of
             that pin's 3V jumper (MO is the motor permit). A4 drives nothing; A5 and 7 open: no job, no fire.
With no relay on the bench, MO drives the node's permit directly: the firmware checks are the only layer.
