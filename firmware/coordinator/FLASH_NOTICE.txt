align-coordinator: interlock, moves, measurement, alignment (M5), PROVISIONAL pins for an Adafruit Feather ESP32-C6

Motors:      moved only by the console's `jog`, `calibrate`, `align`, and `poke` (the last three run the alignment
             procedure one move at a time, its axes started from the nodes' STATUS). Every move goes through the
             state machine of reference 7.3: in Idle with the node present, after 500 ms without a fire, the relay
             is energized; once its feedback reads the laser loop open, in a later millisecond than the energize,
             INHIBIT 0 goes out and then one MOVE_REL with `finish_plus` clear. When the node answers (or not, after
             3 tries), INHIBIT 1 goes out and the relay is released; an answer of a soft limit or a lost rail then
             faults. Every other INHIBIT says inhibited.
             A node that hears this heartbeat refuses console moves (bench moves need a silent bus).
             On every node that appears or restarts it sets each axis's soft limits (-4400 to 4400 steps) and
             direction sign (forward) by CONFIG.
Interlock:   GPIO 22 (Feather MO) drives the relay, high to energize; low at boot, at reset, and in every state but
             arming and moving. GPIO 21 (SCK) reads its feedback, pulled down, high when the laser loop is open.
             A fault (fire or job while armed, feedback disagreeing, a lost node, a node's pin fault, lost rail or
             move timeout, a soft limit, CAN bus-off) releases the relay at once and sends ESTOP, again with every
             heartbeat until the console's `clear`. Losing the node of a move inhibits too.
Ruida I/O:   GPIO 3 (A4) drives FootSW's optocoupler, high = active: high for up to 200 ms only for a measurement
             (`measure`, or one the procedure takes 200 ms after its last move) in Idle, with the relay released
             and the sensor node present. On the machine this starts the Ruida's job, which fires the laser. It goes
             low again as soon as the job stops waiting to start, and is low at boot, at reset, and otherwise; no
             move is armed while it is high. GPIO 2 (A5) reads Status, pulled up, low = job running; GPIO 7 reads
             fire sense, pulled up, low = firing, and latches its edges by interrupt. Both polarities UNVERIFIED
             (hand-off open item 9). Each burst of fire is a SHOT to every node.
Bus:         TWAI through an SN65HVD230 transceiver: TX on GPIO 23 (MI) to CTX, RX on GPIO 1 (A0) from CRX.
             250 kbit/s, normal mode. A heartbeat and the inhibit level every 100 ms, the inhibit at once on change.
Other I/O:   none. Every other GPIO stays in its reset state, among them the Feather's RGB LED and I2C power
             (GPIO 20), its I2C pins (18, 19), and its LED (15).
Flash:       nothing is written after boot.
Watchdog:    the TIMG1 watchdog resets the chip if not fed for 1 s; a reset releases the relay and FootSW.

Bench wiring: SN65HVD230: 3V3 to 3V, GND to GND, CTX to MI, CRX to A0; CANH and CANL to the node's.
             Jumper MO to SCK (the relay's feedback follows its drive), and wire MO to the node's pin 6 in place of
             that pin's 3V jumper (MO is the motor permit). A4 drives nothing; A5 and 7 open: no job, no fire.
With no relay on the bench, MO drives the node's permit directly: the firmware checks are the only layer.
