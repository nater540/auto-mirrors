align-coordinator: the bus only (milestone M5, first step), PROVISIONAL pins for an Adafruit Feather ESP32-C6

Motors:      none moved. It sends no MOVE_REL, no CONFIG, and never INHIBIT 0: every INHIBIT it sends says
             inhibited. A node that hears its heartbeat also refuses console moves (bench moves need a silent bus).
Interlock:   not driven. No relay output exists yet; the motor permit is not touched.
Ruida I/O:   none. FootSW is not driven; Status and fire sense are not read.
Bus:         TWAI through an SN65HVD230 transceiver: TX on GPIO 23 (Feather MI) to CTX, RX on GPIO 1 (A0) from
             CRX. 250 kbit/s, normal mode. It sends a heartbeat and INHIBIT 1 every 100 ms, and reads the nodes'
             heartbeats, STATUS, FAULT, and MOVE_DONE.
Other I/O:   none. Every other GPIO stays in its reset state, among them the Feather's RGB LED and I2C power
             (GPIO 20), its I2C pins (18, 19), and its LED (15).
Flash:       nothing is written after boot.

Bench wiring: SN65HVD230: 3V3 to 3V, GND to GND, CTX to MI, CRX to A0; CANH and CANL to the node's.
The console (`help`) shows each node present or not, and what it last reported.
